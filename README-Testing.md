# Pretty Wide Quest Log // Test Plan

Pretty Wide Quest Log turns the quest log into one wide window, with your whole quest list beside the quest details and a map of the selected quest. This time we most want you to try the new dungeon quest announcements and sharing.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. In a party, walk into a dungeon you have a few quests for (a class quest too, if you have one); after a few seconds your party should see which ones you still need and be offered each one you can share.
2. Walk out and back in, or reset the dungeon, and check nothing repeats; then have someone join or leave the party and check the quests are shared again.
3. Type `/pwql`, open **Quality of Life**, and turn off **Automatic Dungeon Quest Announcements** and then **Automatic Dungeon Quest Sharing**, checking each stops on its own in a dungeon with a new group.
4. In a raid or battleground, share a quest with someone too far away; no whisper should go out, though one still does in a party.
5. Select a quest in a zone you've never been to and check the map shows the whole zone, not bare parchment, with Leatrix Maps turned off too.
6. Turn off **Quest Giver Window Cleanup** under **Quality of Life** and check quest givers go back to Blizzard's look; on WoW Forever, check the quest log keeps its own look either way.

## Everything Else

7. Type `/pwql`, change **Zone Order** and **Quest Order** under **Quest Log**, then press `L` and click and Shift + Click quests to check each click lands on the quest you clicked.
8. With Questie on, select a quest and check the map shows only its icons, using the dropdown in the map's top-left corner for quests in more than one zone.
9. With Questie on, select a quest that's part of a chain and check the details show the chain with your step highlighted.
10. In a party, right-click a zone name in the quest list and click **Share Quests**; your party should get that zone's quests one at a time.
11. Try **Collapse All** and **Track All** above the quest list, then drag the grip in the bottom-right corner to make the log taller.
12. Open the options with `/pwql` and from **Options > AddOns > Pretty Wide Quest Log**; TBC Anniversary is the one that tends to break here.
13. In combat, `/pwql` should refuse to open the options while the quest log still works; on WoW Forever, also click a quest in your tracker mid-fight.
