# Pretty Wide Quest Log // Manual Test Plan

This is the manual test plan for Pretty Wide Quest Log, the steps to confirm it works before a release is tagged. For what it does, see [README.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Pretty-Wide-Quest-Log/blob/main/README.md); for how it works, see [README-Technical.md](https://github.com/Gogo1951/Pretty-Wide-Quest-Log/blob/main/README-Technical.md).

## Before you start

**Run the whole list on each flavor in turn: Classic Era, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** Steps are numbered continuously so you can report "failed on step N."

Gather these once on each client so you aren't caught short mid-run:

- **A character with a well-stocked quest log**: at least eight quests spread over at least three zones, at a spread of levels, with at least six of them showing objectives under their name. Among them you need one quest with a count objective (such as "kill 8"), one quest that is already complete, one quest that rewards gold, experience and an item, and one whose description runs to two paragraphs. A dungeon, group or elite quest is a bonus, since it shows the type letter.
- **A quest giver nearby** you can talk to, one with a quest to offer or to turn in. Best of all is one waiting on a quest that asks you to bring items, so you see the page that lists the required items.
- **Something to fight**, any open-world mob.
- **Questie, if you play with it, with its tracker on.** Steps 2 and 19 use it on every client. On WoW Forever the tracking steps must then follow Questie's tracker instead of Blizzard's; without Questie, they follow Blizzard's tracker.
- **A voice-over add-on with a voice pack**, for steps 24 and 25: VoiceOver on Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, and Spoken Quests (rustykey's Spoken Player) on WoW Forever. Spoken Quests also works on Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, so either one will do there.
- **A non-English client**, only for the optional last step.

No second player is needed, because the add-on never sends anything to other players. Unless a step says otherwise, be **out of combat**, standing in the open world, with every setting at its default. Settings changes take effect the next time the quest log opens, so after changing one, close the Options window and press the quest log key (`L` by default) to look.

This plan deliberately skips Share Quest (it needs a group), the greyed-out Track button and Track All's quiet skip for quests missing from Questie's database (there is no dependable quest to test them with), a greyed-out Track All (it needs a log with nothing left to track or untrack), the warning that Wide Quest Log Plus is also turned on, ElvUI's skin, the Feedback & Support link boxes, the stock profile copy and delete controls, and every Diagnostic Tools report except Quest Log Context and API Endpoints.

## Verify this release's changes

**Expand All and Track All**

**1.** Open the quest log. Above the quest list, at the top left where the old **All** +/- toggle used to be, two buttons must sit side by side: **Collapse All** and **Track All** (or **Untrack All**, if every quest is already tracked), the same width, each label fully inside its button. No **All** toggle may show behind or beside them. Click **Collapse All**: every zone must collapse and the button must now read **Expand All**, without changing width. Click it: every zone must open again. Collapse every zone one at a time by clicking their names: once the last one closes, the button must read **Expand All**. This closes the change that replaced the **All** toggle with these two buttons. Failure is the old toggle still showing or clickable, a button that grows or shrinks as its label changes, a cut-off label, a zone left collapsed, or a label that doesn't match the zones.

**2.** Shift + Click quests until none is tracked (or click **Untrack All** if it shows): the button must read **Track All**. Click it: every quest showing objectives must be tracked, its mark changing in the list, and appear in your tracker (Questie's, if it's on), and the button must then read **Untrack All**. **On Classic Era and TBC Anniversary without Questie, Blizzard's tracker holds only five quests: the five highest in the list must be tracked, a red message must say you can't track more, and the button must read Untrack All. A quest that only sends you to talk to someone may stay untracked there, just as Shift + Click leaves it.** Click **Untrack All**: every quest must leave the tracker, including Questie's, and the button must read **Track All** again. Failure is a quest left out with room to spare, the wrong five tracked, quests still in a tracker after Untrack All, a label that doesn't change, or a Lua error.

**Quest text**

**3.** Open the quest log and select the quest with gold, experience and an item, then the one whose description runs to two paragraphs. The quest's name must be the biggest heading, with **Description** and **Rewards** in the same lettering but visibly smaller. Quest text must have a little space between its lines, the same on every client, with a blank line between paragraphs and a clearly bigger gap above **Description** and **Rewards**. The reward buttons must sit clear of the line above them (*"You will be able to choose one of these rewards:"*) and of the line below them. On WoW Forever, the experience number must be in the same dark lettering as the objective counts, not white with an outline. At the bottom right, a smaller line must read `Quest ID` and the quest's number, such as `Quest ID 5725`. Failure is headings all the same size, lines of text touching, reward buttons touching the text, or `ID` without `Quest`.

**4.** Talk to the quest giver and step through its pages (offering a quest, asking for items, and turning one in, where you can). The quest's name, the smaller section headings (such as **Quest Objectives** and **Rewards**) and the line spacing must look the same as in the quest log, with the experience number in the same dark lettering as the text, and every word must still fit with the reward buttons in their usual places. On every client, the page asking for required items must have the same space above and below **Required items:** as the quest log has around its headings, and `Quest ID` with the quest's number at the bottom right under the last item. On WoW Forever, the offer and turn-in pages must also show the gold and experience at the right and `Quest ID` at the bottom right; scroll down on a long quest to check it can be reached. **The quest giver window draws with its own pieces on Classic Era and TBC Anniversary and with the quest log's on WoW Forever, so check it on all three.** On Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, open the quest log first: it must make way for the quest giver window the way Blizzard's own does, and once you close the quest giver window its details must still be there. Failure is text cut off or overlapping, the quest giver window and the quest log looking different, no quest ID on the required items page, or quest details left blank.

**Zone gaps and the untracked mark**

**5.** Open the quest log. Above every zone name except the first must be a gap of about half a row: clearly more than the space between two quest rows, but less than a whole empty row. Scroll to the very bottom: the last quest must still be reachable and fully visible. Untrack one quest: its crossed-out eye must be the same deep red as the minus buttons on the zone names, not a bright red. **On Classic Era and TBC Anniversary the gaps move Blizzard's own rows, so check there that no row is drawn on top of another or past the bottom edge.** Failure is a full blank row, no gap, a last quest you can't reach, overlapping rows, or a bright red eye.

**WoW Forever's window**

**6.** On WoW Forever only, open the quest log. The buttons along the bottom must read **Track** (or **Untrack**) then **Abandon Quest** at the left, and **Share Quest** then **Exit** at the right, each label inside its button. Select a quest and click **Track**: it must toggle tracking for that quest. Failure is the old order (Track beside Share Quest), a cut-off label, or a button acting on the wrong quest.

**7.** On WoW Forever only, open the quest log, then talk to the quest giver: the quest log must close as its window opens, and the quest giver window must show its text and rewards as normal, not blank. Do the same with a gossip window, a vendor and the character pane (`C`): each time the quest log must close. Open your bags (`B`) instead: the quest log must stay open. Talk to the quest giver again and, with its window open, press `L`: the quest giver window must close and the quest log must open with the selected quest's details showing, not a blank page. **WoW Forever is the client where the quest giver window and the quest log draw their details with the same pieces.** Failure is the quest log left open on top of or under a Blizzard window, the quest log closing for your bags, or a blank quest giver window or quest log.

**WoW Forever's map**

**8.** On WoW Forever only, press `L`. A map must sit to the right of the quest log, its top edge level with the quest log's, about two thirds as tall, with the zone's name across its top and a close button at its top right. Select the quest with a count objective: the map must switch to that quest's zone, with its objective area shaded and quest markers on it. Select a quest in another zone: the map must follow. **With Questie installed, choose Questie's own objectives in its options first: the map must still show Blizzard's shaded areas and markers.** Failure is no map, a map overlapping the quest log, a map that stays on one zone, or no shaded area for a quest that has objectives.

**9.** On WoW Forever only, open the quest log. Between **Abandon Quest** and **Share Quest** a button must read **Hide Map**, its label inside the button. Click it: the map must close and the button must read **Show Map**. Close the quest log, open it again, then `/reload` and open it again: the map must stay closed. Click **Show Map**: the map must come back. Close it with its own close button: the button must read **Show Map** again. Failure is a map that comes back by itself, a label that doesn't match, or a cut-off label.

**10.** On WoW Forever only, with the map showing, drag the quest log's resize grip down: the map must grow with it, redrawn to fill its frame, with no blank strip. Scroll the mouse wheel over the map: it must zoom in and out. Press `M`: the world map must open as normal, with Blizzard's own quest list beside it. Failure is a map that keeps its old size, a blank strip, or a changed world map.

**Options description and API checks**

**11.** Type `/wide`: the line at the top of the panel must read *"A bigger, smarter quest log that gives you more room to read, more information at a glance, and control over how your quests are organized. Keep the familiar Blizzard look, just make the quest log work better for you."* Open **Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, open the **Code** tab, and click **Run** beside **API Endpoints**: every row must read `[PASS]`, ending on *"Every API is present on this client."* Failure is the old description, any `[FAIL]` row (note which), or a Lua error.

When steps 1-11 pass on every flavor, this release's changes are verified. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.

## Core checks

**12.** At character select, open the **AddOns** list: Pretty Wide Quest Log must appear **once**, spelled with spaces, with the quest log book icon, not flagged out of date. Log in, then type `/reload`. Both times there must be no Lua error window and no red error text, and a line must print in the shape *"Pretty Wide Quest Log // Version ... Settings (including the option to disable this message) can be found under Options > AddOns > Pretty Wide Quest Log. Enjoying the add-on? Tell a friend about it! (="*. Type `/wide`, untick **Enable Welcome Message**, and `/reload`: no line may print. Tick it again and `/reload`: the line must be back. Failure is any error naming Pretty Wide Quest Log, no line, a line that ignores the toggle, or `nil`, `%s` or a raw key such as `CHAT_LOADED` in it.

**13.** Type `/wide`. The settings must appear **docked inside the Blizzard Options window**, with Pretty Wide Quest Log selected in the category list on the left. Close the window, then press `Esc`, choose **Options**, then **AddOns**, and select **Pretty Wide Quest Log**. The same docked panel must appear, with three entries under it in this order: **Pretty Wide Quest Log**, **Profiles**, **Diagnostic Tools**, each opening without error. The add-on has no mini-map button, so `/wide` and this list are every way in. Failure looks like either nothing happening at all, a standalone window floating free of the Options frame, or a missing, blank or misordered entry. **TBC Anniversary is the flavor that historically breaks this, so a tester who runs only Classic Era has not finished.**

**14.** Pull a mob and, while still in combat, type `/wide`. Chat must print *"As a safety precaution, the Options Interface cannot be opened during combat."* and the panel must **not** open. Finish the fight and wait: the panel must not open by itself afterwards. Failure is the panel opening, silence, or a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error.

**15.** Press `L`. The quest log must open as one wide window: the quest list on the left and the selected quest's details on the right, with no seams or gaps in the parchment art. Every quest row must start with its level in brackets, such as `[14]`, with a letter after the level for a dungeon, raid, PvP, group or elite quest (`[14D]`, `[60R]`, `[30P]`, `[22G]`, `[56E]`). Your complete quest must say `(Complete)` at the right of its row. Close it and click the quest log button on the micro menu: the same window must open. **On WoW Forever, also press `M`: the world map must still show Blizzard's own quest list beside it.** Failure is a narrow, single-pane log, missing levels, titles running into their tags, or the micro menu button opening something else.

**16.** Select the quest with a count objective. Each objective must show a green check when done (and a blank of the same width when not, so names line up), with its count, such as `3 / 8`, lined up in a column on the right. Select the quest with gold, experience and an item: the gold and the experience must line up on the right in the same way, and the item must sit in the rewards. Failure is a count overlapping its objective's name, or numbers running past the edge of the parchment.

**17.** Type `/wide`: **Zone Order** must read **Average Level, Highest First (Default)** and **Quest Order** **Level, Highest First (Default)**. In the quest log, the zone whose quests average the highest level must sit at the top with the rest in falling order, each zone's quests highest level first. Set **Zone Order** to **Average Level, Lowest First**: the zone order must flip. Set it to **Alphabetical**: zones must run A to Z by full name (so "The ..." files under T). Set **Quest Order** to **Level, Lowest First**, then **Alphabetical**: within every zone the quests must follow. Untick **Space Above Zone Names**: the gaps must go, and come back when you tick it, with no `/reload`. **On Classic Era and TBC Anniversary the add-on redraws Blizzard's rows in its own order, so check there that every row shows the right quest and none is drawn twice.** Failure is an order matching neither the levels nor the names, a setting that changes nothing, or a quest in the wrong zone.

**18.** Leave **Quest Order** on **Alphabetical**. Click a quest that isn't first in its zone: the details and the highlight must be for **that** quest. Open your chat box and Shift + Click another quest: a link to **that** quest must go into the chat box. Close the chat box and Shift + Click an untracked quest: **that** row's mark must change and the quest must join your tracker; Shift + Click it again to put it back. Click a zone name: **that** zone must collapse. **This matters most on Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, where sorting redraws Blizzard's own rows, so a row can show one quest while acting on another.** Failure is any click landing on a different quest or zone, or Shift + Click toggling tracking while the chat box is open.

**19.** Type `/wide` and hover **Mark Untracked Quests**: the tooltip must read "Tracked quests lose their checkmark, and untracked quests get an eye instead. Handy if you track almost everything.", with the teal check and the red eye drawn in it. It must be ticked. In the quest log, tracked quests must show nothing in front of their name and each untracked quest the eye, lined up under the zone names' +/-, every title just after it. Untick the option: teal checks must be back on the tracked quests and the eyes gone, with no `/reload`. Tick it again. **On Classic Era and TBC Anniversary the mark is drawn in Blizzard's own check, so scroll the list too and check that no row keeps the wrong mark.** With Questie's tracker on, log out, log back in and open the quest log at once: no row may flash an eye; the marks may be missing for a few seconds, then must appear on only the untracked quests by themselves. Failure is a blank-square icon (the game was not restarted after install), both marks or neither on a row, or a mark that ignores the toggle.

**20.** Hover the grip in the quest log's bottom-right corner: a tooltip must read *"Drag to resize the quest log"*. Drag it down: the window must grow in whole rows, the list must show more quests, and the art must stretch with no seams. Close the log, `/reload`, and reopen it: it must keep that height. Type `/wide`, click **Reset Size and Position**, and accept *"Reset the quest log to its default size and position?"*: the log must be back to its default height (and, on WoW Forever, its starting place near the top left). Failure is the art tearing, the height forgotten after the reload, no prompt, or the reset changing nothing.

**21.** On WoW Forever only, drag the quest log by its title bar to a new place, `/reload`, and reopen it: it must open where you left it. Press `Esc`: it must close. Collapse the zone of a tracked quest, close the log, and click that quest in your objective tracker (or Questie's): the log must open with the zone reopened and that quest selected and scrolled into view. **WoW Forever is the only client where the add-on builds its own window, so these paths exist nowhere else.** Failure is a forgotten position, `Esc` not closing it, or a tracker click opening the world map or the wrong quest.

**22.** On WoW Forever only, type `/wide`: **Enable Pretty Wide Quest Log for This Profile** must sit directly under **Enable Welcome Message**, ticked. Untick it: the whole **Quest Log** section must vanish. Close the Options window: a popup must offer to reload. Click **Reload UI** and press `L`: Blizzard's quest list in the world map must open instead, from the micro menu button too. Talk to the quest giver: its window must still have the smaller headings and line spacing from step 4. Tick the toggle again, move to **Profiles** in the category list, and close from there: the popup must still appear; reload, and `L` must open the add-on's window. Toggling it twice before closing must bring no popup. **On Classic Era and TBC Anniversary the toggle must not appear at all, and closing Options must never prompt.** Failure is the toggle on the wrong clients, a missing or unwanted popup, or `L` ignoring the setting after the reload.

**23.** Set both orders away from their defaults, untick **Space Above Zone Names**, **Mark Untracked Quests** and **Enable Welcome Message**, and drag the quest log taller. Open **Profiles** and click **Reset Profile**. Back on the main panel, both orders must read **(Default)** and all three toggles must be ticked, with no `/reload`. The quest log must **keep** its taller size, because a profile reset never moves or resizes the window. Failure is any setting surviving the reset, stale values until a reload, or the window snapping back.

**24.** On Classic Era and TBC Anniversary, with VoiceOver or Spoken Quests on, open the quest log. Every quest row must show a play button in front of its title, with the level and title clear of it, and no button on a zone name or a gap. Click the play button on a quest that isn't first in its zone, then on one lower down after scrolling: each reading must be for **that** quest. A quest with no recording must show its button greyed out. Set **Zone Order** to **Alphabetical** and **Quest Order** to **Level, Lowest First**: every quest must keep its button. With Spoken Quests, switch off its quest log buttons in its own settings and `/reload`: the buttons must be gone, with no empty space before the titles. Failure is a button over the level or title, a reading for a different quest, a button on the wrong row after scrolling, or a gap left once the buttons are off.

**25.** On WoW Forever, with Spoken Quests on, open the quest log. Every quest row must show a play button where the title would start, with the title moved over to clear it, and a long title must shorten before it runs into `(Complete)`. Click the play button on a quest that isn't first in its zone: the reading must be for **that** quest. Collapse and reopen a zone, scroll, and change **Quest Order**: each button must stay with its own quest, never doubled or on a zone name or gap. Press `M`: the world map's own list must still show Spoken Quests' buttons. Close it and press `L`: the add-on's buttons must still be there. Failure is no buttons, a button over the title, a reading for a different quest, a stray or doubled button, or buttons vanishing from either list.

**26.** Pull a mob and, while in combat, press `L`, click a quest, Shift + Click it to toggle tracking, click **Collapse All** and **Expand All**, and close the log. Everything must work exactly as out of combat. **On WoW Forever, where the add-on takes over the quest log key, also click a quest in your tracker during the fight.** Failure is the log refusing to open, a red `ADDON_ACTION_BLOCKED` error, or a Lua error.

**27.** Open **Diagnostic Tools**, tick **Enable Diagnostic Tools**, open the **Settings** tab, and click **Run** beside **Quest Log Context**. The first line must end with `Flavor` and `Data` naming your client: `Vanilla` on Classic Era, `Camelot` on WoW Forever, `TBC` on TBC Anniversary (a Season of Discovery realm reads `Flavor Vanilla (Season of Discovery) // Data Discovery`). Below it, the orders, zone spacing and welcome message must match the main panel, and Questie, ElvUI, VoiceOver and Spoken Quests must each read `loaded` or `not loaded` correctly. Then `/reload` and open **Diagnostic Tools** again: **Enable Diagnostic Tools** must be **off**, with only the warning paragraph and the toggle visible. Failure is a Lua error, `nil` or another client's name in the header, settings that don't match, or the toggle still on after the reload.

**28.** Optional, on a non-English client. Log in: the welcome line must print in that language. Type `/wide`: every label and tooltip must render in that language. Open the quest log: **Expand All**, **Collapse All**, **Track All** and **Untrack All** must be translated and fit their buttons, type letters may be translated, and the `Quest ID` line must show a real number. Failure is a raw key such as `ZONE_ORDER` or `TRACK_ALL` on screen, `nil` or a stray `%s` or `%d` anywhere, or text running off the panel or out of a button.

When every step passes on each of Classic Era, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary, manual testing is complete. Proceed to `4 - Pre-Launch Review Prompt.md`.
