# Pretty Wide Quest Log // Test Plan

Pretty Wide Quest Log turns the quest log into one wide window, with your whole quest list beside the quest details and a map of the selected quest. This time we most want you to try the new whispers when a quest share fails, and the new Feedback & Support page.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. In a party, share a few quests with someone too far away; they should get one full whisper, then a short **Same goes for** line for each quest after it, and the full whisper again once you leave and rejoin a group.
2. Type `/pwql` and click **Feedback & Support**; the four links and the version number should be there, and no longer on the main page.

## Everything Else

3. Type `/pwql`, change **Zone Order** and **Quest Order** under **Quest Log**, then press `L` and click and Shift + Click quests to check each click lands on the quest you clicked.
4. With Questie on, select a quest and check the map shows only its icons, using the dropdown in the map's top-left corner for quests in more than one zone.
5. With Questie on, select a quest that's part of a chain and check the details show the chain with your step highlighted.
6. Select a quest in a zone you've never been to and check the map shows the whole zone, not bare parchment, with Leatrix Maps turned off too.
7. In a party, walk into a dungeon you have a few quests for; your party should see which ones you still need, once, and be offered each one you can share.
8. In a party, right-click a zone name in the quest list and click **Share 3 Quests** (or however many it offers); your party should get that zone's quests one at a time.
9. Try **Collapse All** and **Track All** above the quest list, then drag the grip in the bottom-right corner to make the log taller.
10. Turn off **Quest Giver Window Cleanup** under **Quality of Life** and check quest givers go back to Blizzard's look.
11. Open the options with `/pwql` and from **Options > AddOns > Pretty Wide Quest Log**; TBC Anniversary is the one that tends to break here.
12. In combat, `/pwql` should refuse to open the options while the quest log still works; on WoW Forever, also click a quest in your tracker mid-fight.
