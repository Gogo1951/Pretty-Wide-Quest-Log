# Pretty Wide Quest Log // Test Plan

Pretty Wide Quest Log turns the quest log into one wide window, with your whole quest list beside the quest details and a map of the selected quest. This time we most want you to try the reorganized Quest Log options and the quest map with Questie.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, TBC Anniversary, and Mists of Pandaria Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Type `/pwql`, open **Quest Log**, and try each click listed under **Handy Clicks**.
2. Scroll the mouse wheel over the map beside the quest log; neither the map nor your camera should zoom.
3. With Questie on, select a quest with objectives inside a dungeon; the map should show a zone, never a blank map.
4. When you're not in a party, hover the grayed-out **Share Quest** button; it should tell you why.
5. With ElvUI on, select your first quest after logging in; its quest ID should match the color of the description.
6. Turn off **Enable Wide Quest Log** under **Quest Log**, then open **Quality of Life**; **Quest Sharing** should say right-clicking a zone name needs it, then turn it back on.

## Everything Else

7. Press `L`, change **Zone Order** and **Quest Order** under **Quest Log**, then click and Shift-Click quests to check each click lands on the quest you clicked.
8. With Questie on, select a quest that has you kill or collect things; the map should shade each area with one icon in it, and a quest in a chain should list its steps.
9. On WoW Forever and Mists of Pandaria Classic, press `L` in and out of combat, and out of combat click the quest log button on the micro menu and a quest in the objective tracker; each should open the wide quest log.
10. In a party, walk into a dungeon you have a few quests for; your party should see which ones you still need, once, and be offered each one you can share.
11. In a party, right-click a zone name in the quest list and click **Share 3 Quests** (or however many it offers); anyone too far away should get a whisper naming the quest.
12. Open the options with `/pwql` and from **Options > AddOns > Pretty Wide Quest Log**.
13. In combat, `/pwql` should refuse to open the options with a chat message, while the quest log still opens and scrolls.
