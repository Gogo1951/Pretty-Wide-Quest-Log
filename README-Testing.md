# Pretty Wide Quest Log // Test Plan

Pretty Wide Quest Log turns the quest log into one wide window, with quest levels and tags and your whole quest list beside the quest details. This time we most want you to try the map beside the quest log, which now shows Questie's icons for just the quest you pick.

**Play on Classic Era, WoW Forever, and TBC Anniversary.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. With Questie on, press `L` and select a quest: the map beside the log should show only that quest's icons, a little larger than on Questie's world map.
2. Select a quest with objectives in more than one zone and switch between them with the dropdown in the map's top-left corner.
3. Hover an icon on the map to see the quest and your progress, then kill something for that quest and watch the icons update.
4. Without Questie on Classic Era or TBC Anniversary, check the map shows the zone you're in.
5. Click **Hide Map**, `/reload`, and check the map stays hidden until you click **Show Map**.
6. On Classic Era and TBC Anniversary with ElvUI, check the quest log, its buttons and the map all wear ElvUI's look, with nothing of Questie's tracker showing through.

## Everything Else

7. Press `L` and check the quest log opens as one wide window, with each quest's level and tags in front of its name.
8. Type `/wide`, change **Zone Order** and **Quest Order**, then click and Shift + Click quests to check each click lands on the quest you clicked.
9. Try **Collapse All** and **Track All** above the quest list, then drag the grip in the bottom-right corner to make the log taller.
10. Talk to a quest giver and check its window matches the quest log's lettering and spacing.
11. Open the options with `/wide` and from **Options > AddOns > Pretty Wide Quest Log**; TBC Anniversary is the one that tends to break here.
12. In combat, `/wide` should refuse to open the options, but the quest log should still open and work; on WoW Forever, also click a quest in your tracker mid-fight.
13. With VoiceOver or Spoken Quests on, click a few quests' play buttons and check each reads its own quest.
