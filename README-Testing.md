# Pretty Wide Quest Log // Test Plan

Pretty Wide Quest Log turns the quest log into one wide window and, with Questie on, draws a route on the zone map with a Quest Arrow to follow. This time we most want you to try the new zone discs on the continent and world maps, and putting another zone's quests on your route from its map.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, WoW Forever, TBC Anniversary, and MoP Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. With Questie on, open the continent map; each zone with quests to pick up should have a colored disc that counts them by color when you hover it and opens that zone's map when you click it.
2. With Questie on, open the map of a zone you're not in, right-click a quest to pick up to **Add to the Route**; it should join your route once you get there.
3. With Questie on and **Plan Ahead** chosen under **What to Route**, press **M**; quests to pick up should show in their level color, with no Questie **!** icons or markers from the zones next door.
4. Hover the Quest Arrow; the **Route** row should name your **What to Route** choice, with a check beside that choice in the menu it opens.
5. Hover a marker on the zone map; its menu should open with the quest's name at the top and stay open if your cursor slips off for a moment.
6. On WoW Forever, turn on the game's Instant Quest Text and talk to a quest giver; the quest's whole text should show, not just its first few letters.
7. Turn off **Enable Wide Quest Log**, change a setting on the **Quest List** page, then turn it back on and reload; your change should be in effect.
8. Under **Window & Map** and the Quest Arrow options, **Reset Window** and **Arrow Position** should stay gray until you move the quest log or the arrow.

## Everything Else

9. Press **L** and change **Zone Order** and **Quest Order** under **Quest List** in the options; the list should re-sort.
10. With Questie on, select a quest that has you kill or collect things; the map beside the log should shade each area, and a quest in a chain should list its steps.
11. With Questie on, press **M** and follow the Quest Arrow through a zone's quests, turn-ins included.
12. In a party, hover a zone name in the quest log and click **Share Quests**; your party should be offered each quest they don't have.
13. On WoW Forever and MoP Classic, press **L**, use a Quest Arrow item button, and share a quest, all **in combat**; WoW Forever should share it once combat ends, MoP Classic right away.
14. Open the options with `/pwql` and from **Options > AddOns > Pretty Wide Quest Log**, then try `/pwql` in combat; it should refuse with a chat message.
